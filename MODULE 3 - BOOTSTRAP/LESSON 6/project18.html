🌿 Rainforest Adventure

The rainforest is a rich and lively habitat filled with tall trees, colourful birds, and hidden animals. It is warm, wet, and bursting with life, making it one of the most exciting places on Earth.

🐊 Jungle River Journey

The jungle river is full of movement and mystery, with flowing water, shady banks, and many creatures living nearby. Frogs, fish, turtles, and crocodiles depend on this river for food, shelter, and survival.

🌙 Night Jungle Safari

At night, the jungle becomes quiet but alive with glowing fireflies, watching owls, and sneaky hunters moving through the darkness. The moonlight creates a mysterious world where animals adapt to the cool night air.
