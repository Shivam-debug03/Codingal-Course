🎤 Michael Jackson

The King of Pop - A Legacy That Changed Music Forever

Michael Joseph Jackson (August 29, 1958 – June 25, 2009)

Born in Gary, Indiana | Passed in Los Angeles, California

Michael Joseph Jackson was an American singer, songwriter, and dancer. Dubbed the King of Pop, he is regarded as one of the most significant cultural figures of the 20th century.

Jackson's contributions to music, dance, and fashion, along with his publicized personal life, made him a global figure in popular culture for over four decades.

Quick Facts

First Solo #1

At age 13, Michael scored his first solo top 10 hit with "Ben" in 1972.

Grammy Record

He won 8 Grammy Awards in a single night for Thriller, tying the record at the time.

Oscar Winner

He won an Academy Award for Best Original Song Score for the film Escape.

Global Impact

His tours were among the highest-grossing live concert events in the world.

The Early Years & The Jackson 5

🌟 Beginnings

He started performing as a child prodigy and became the lead singer of his family's group, the Jackson 5, with Motown hits like "I Want You Back".

🚀 Breakthrough

The Jackson 5's success paved the way for his solo career, which began in the early 1970s.

Solo Career & Major Achievements

💿 The Thriller Phenomenon

After going solo, his 1982 album Thriller became—and remains—the best-selling album of all time.

🎬 Visual Storytelling

He revolutionized the medium of music videos with cinematic and complex productions for songs like Billie Jean, Beat It, and Thriller.

Dance, Style & Legacy

💃 Signature Moves

He popularized intricate dance routines, most notably his famous glide known as the Moonwalk.

✨ Iconic Trademarks

Known for his signature aesthetic, including a single sequined white glove, black fedora, and white socks with black shoes.

Humanitarianism & Activism

Beyond music, he actively supported humanitarian causes and wrote benefit songs like "We Are the World" (with Lionel Richie) to raise millions for famine relief.

"If you wanna make the world a better place, take a look at yourself, and make the change."
