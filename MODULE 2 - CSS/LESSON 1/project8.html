Son Goku is the cheerful, Saiyan-born protagonist of the Dragon Ball series. Originally sent to Earth as a baby to destroy it, a childhood head injury erased his destructive programming. He evolved into a martial arts prodigy, dedicating his life to protecting his adopted home and constantly pushing his physical limits.
